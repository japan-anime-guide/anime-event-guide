About this guide

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Japan Anime Event Guide is an English guide to anime exhibitions, pop-up shops, collaboration cafés and limited-edition goods in Japan. It is made for visitors who want to know what is on during their trip, where it is, and how to get there.

Who runs it

The guide is run independently by a Japan-based anime fan who also makes the short videos on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram. It is not affiliated with any studio, publisher, brand, retailer or venue listed here, and no one pays to be included.

What we list

Only things a traveler can actually go to or buy in Japan: exhibitions, pop-up stores, café and restaurant collaborations, nationwide chain campaigns, lottery prizes and similar. Events that have ended are removed.

How we check the details

  1. Dates, opening hours, prices and venues are taken from the official website or press release.
  2. Each event is also cross-checked against at least one independent news report.
  3. Anything we cannot confirm is marked “TBA” instead of guessed. Each event shows the date it was last checked.

Schedules in Japan can change at short notice, so please confirm on the official link before you travel.

How events are ranked

The “Recommended” order and the PICK UP events on the front page are based on a simple score: how well known the series is internationally, how widely the news was covered, how soon the event starts or ends, and how easy it is to visit without a reservation.

How often it is updated

New events are added and finished ones removed every two days.

Images

Event images come from official press materials and are used to introduce the events. All rights belong to their respective owners. If you are a rights holder and want an image changed or removed, please contact us and we will act promptly.

How the site is funded

The guide is free to use. It currently has no ads. If we add affiliate links in the future, they will be clearly labeled “PR” and listed on the Privacy & Disclosures page.
