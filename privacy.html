Privacy & Disclosures

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Who runs this site

Japan Anime Event Guide is an independent guide run by the creator of the tanjiro_animeivent_injapan video channels. It is not affiliated with any studio, brand, retailer or venue listed.

What we collect

This site has no accounts, forms or comments, and it does not use analytics. We do not collect your name, email address or any other personal information.

Two outside services see basic technical data when you open the page:

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and, like any web host, processes your IP address and browser information to deliver pages and protect against abuse.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces, so your browser requests font files from Google's servers.

Links to official sites, maps and ticket sellers take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.

Advertising and affiliate links

This site currently shows no ads and no affiliate links. Links to booking sites (for example mobile data or airport trains) are ordinary links that we think are useful. If we add affiliate links in the future, each one will be clearly labeled as an advertisement (“PR”), and this page will be updated to name the programs we use.

Accuracy

Dates, hours and prices are checked against official sources on the date shown on each event, but schedules in Japan can change. Always confirm on the official link before you travel.

Images and logos

Event images come from official press materials published by the organizers, and are used to introduce the events. Series logos come from Wikimedia Commons or official press materials. All rights belong to their respective owners.

If you are a rights holder and would like an image removed or corrected, please contact us and we will act promptly.
